About WCRE

WCRE is a local, owner-operated real estate company. We buy properties others pass over, restore them with care, and stay accountable to the neighbors who live next door.

When we sell a home, we want the buyer to be proud of it. When we rent one, we want it to be a place people are glad to come home to. That is what leaving the community better looks like to us.
